# Conversations

Role: `runtime-generated`.

Conversation folders may hold safe current-status artifacts and compact review packets produced by a compatible runtime.

Do not store raw transcripts, secrets, credentials, payment tokens, full private records, or unnecessary personal data here.

Allowed seed artifacts:

- current-status summaries
- owner onboarding summaries
- human review packets
- closeout notes
- vector upsert summaries or references

Private conversation or audience memory belongs behind policy-governed SemFS/vector references.
