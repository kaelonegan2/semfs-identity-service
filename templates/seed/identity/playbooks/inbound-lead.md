# Intake Playbook

Role: `inactive_future`.

Lead or intake qualification is not active in seed mode.

Seed behavior:

- verified owner can ask the identity to draft an intake proposal
- non-owner requests for action, pricing, scheduling, or commitments route to `human_review`
- external sends remain blocked

Future activation requires approved offering/audience context, intake policy, external-send policy, runtime support, and eval coverage.
