# Maturity Model

Role: `guidance`.

## Seed Runtime Available

Active mode: `seed_runtime_available`.

The identity can inspect, summarize, draft, record, propose, create review packets, and upsert safe summaries through policy.

It cannot operate live external workflows.

## Young Future

A young identity may activate limited external-facing drafts after owner approval, eval coverage, approved offering knowledge, and runtime support.

## Mid-State Future

The mid-state target may include lead qualification, service Q&A, estimate packet preparation, work packet preparation, maintenance follow-up, governed vector memory, usage governance, and capability evolution.

## Mature Future

A mature identity may use live tools after explicit owner approval, credential binding, eval coverage, idempotency controls, and audit.
