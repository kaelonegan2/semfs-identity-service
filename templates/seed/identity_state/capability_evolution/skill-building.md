# Skill Building

Role: `guidance` for durable skills.

Skills are durable capability descriptions that may support prompts, tools, specialists, or agents. They are not workflows by themselves.

## Flow

1. Detect repeated task pattern.
2. Describe skill purpose and boundaries.
3. Identify required knowledge, tools, and authority.
4. Draft skill guidance.
5. Add eval coverage.
6. Owner or operations reviewer approves.
7. Connect skill to prompt/tool/specialist/agent as needed.

## Boundary

A skill can be documented before it is executable. Runtime use depends on active prompts, tools, and registries.
