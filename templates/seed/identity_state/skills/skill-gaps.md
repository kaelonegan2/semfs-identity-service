# Skill Gaps

## Live Scheduling

The identity cannot check real calendars, capacity systems, or commit dates.

## Live System Of Record

The identity does not yet write to a CRM, account database, or other production system of record.

## Autonomous Send Policy

External sends still require approval until a governed outbound policy is activated.

## Private Account Or Property Memory Specialist

Private audience, account, property, project, or preference learning is possible by proposal, but a dedicated activated specialist would improve consistency.

## Recurring Plan Recommender

The identity can draft recurring plan concepts, but it cannot yet recommend structured plans from private memory, seasonal needs, or account context without approved capability.
