# Human Review Result Resume

Role: `guidance`.

In seed mode, a human review result may resume only to an active seed route:

- `owner_onboarding`
- `context_collect`
- `research_plan`
- `knowledge_draft`
- `capability_gap`
- `capability_proposal`
- `human_review`
- `stop`

Approval capture records the decision. It does not automatically activate capabilities, tools, agents, specialists, credentials, payments, sends, or policies.
