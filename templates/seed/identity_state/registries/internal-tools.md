# Internal Tools

Role: `guidance`.

Seed agents may use baseline internal tools to mature the identity safely.

Tool use should be invisible to the owner unless a decision is needed. Owner-facing language should describe the business outcome, not the runtime mechanism.

Bad owner question: "Should I create a vector namespace for lead qualification?"

Good owner question: "I recommend keeping early intake notes as internal summaries until you approve external-facing intake. Is that okay?"

## Safe Write Policy

Safe writes are limited to:

- conversation current-status summaries
- draft business profile assumptions
- draft research plans
- draft knowledge candidates
- capability gap records
- inactive capability proposals
- human review packets

Safe writes must not contain secrets, raw private transcripts, live credentials, payment tokens, or unapproved business commitments.
