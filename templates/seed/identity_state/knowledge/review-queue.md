# Knowledge Review Queue

Role: `runtime-generated` seed review queue.

No approved external-facing knowledge exists at seed initialization.

## Seed Draft Format

- Item id.
- Draft question or policy.
- Draft answer.
- Source basis: owner input, public research, inference, or unknown.
- Confidence.
- Risk flags.
- Proposed audience.
- Reviewer role.
- Status.
- Revision notes.

## Initial Draft Targets

- identity purpose summary
- business/function profile assumptions
- offering or responsibility candidates
- audience or operating-area policy candidate
- non-binding pricing or commitment language
- public/user FAQ candidates
- intake question candidates

All current items are `draft_pending_owner_review` until created and approved.
