# Seed To Evergreen Hearth Example

Role: `example`.

This example explains how the generic Solo seed can become a mid-state Evergreen Hearth identity. It is not seed core.

The generic seed starts without a domain. Through owner context, research, drafting, and approval, the identity can become a residential landscaping operating identity around Portland.

## Maturation Sketch

1. Owner says they run or want to run a landscaping business.
2. `context_collector` records that as owner-provided context, not final policy.
3. `business_research_planner` drafts research questions for local service-area language, common services, seasonal needs, and customer concerns.
4. `knowledge_drafter` drafts service categories and FAQ candidates for review.
5. `capability_gap_manager` records gaps for lead qualification, service Q&A, estimate packets, payments, and property memory.
6. `capability_proposal_writer` drafts inactive proposals.
7. Owner approves selected profile, service-area, tone, and authority policies.
8. Runtime support and eval coverage allow selected capabilities to move from proposed to active.

## Example Evergreen-Specific Content

Evergreen Hearth may eventually include landscaping, Portland-area service language, residential customer types, seasonal cleanup, planting refreshes, estimate preparation, and customer property memory.

All such content belongs to the matured example, not the seed package.
